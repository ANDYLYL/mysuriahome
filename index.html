OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT FRAMEWORK

Understanding the SEDA
SuRIA Home Solar Program

The Sustainable Rebate and Incentive Assistance (SuRIA) Home program is launched by the Ministry of Energy Transition and Water Transformation (PETRA) and administered by SEDA Malaysia.

The Government Subsidy

Receive RM600 for every 1kWac of installed solar PV capacity. This program was structured specifically to help middle-class families lower upfront adoption costs.

RM 600 / KWAC CAPACITY

Maximum Funding Cap

The official government entitlement is capped at a maximum of RM3,000 per eligible household. This limit corresponds to a standard 5kWac solar array.

RM 3,000 MAX GOVERNMENT CAP

Domestic Individual Customers

Exclusively open to domestic residential individual low-voltage custom-tariff account holders of Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB) in Peninsular Malaysia.

TNB ACCOUNT MATCH REQUIRED

Important Program Limitations (250MW National Cap)

The SEDA SuRIA Home quota is strictly restricted to a 250MW capped national pool. SEDA and PETRA award slots on a first-come, first-served base until completely exhausted. This allocation covers design approvals, grid interconnect applications, and solar engineering permits.
